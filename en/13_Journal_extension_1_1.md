# Journal — lexical extension 1.1.0

Date: 2026-10-24. Request: develop the most complete dictionary possible from the installed base. Back: [Overview](00_Accueil.md).

## Result

- 2,918 entries, up from 465: 2,453 additions.
- 1,073 new proposed lemmas, 1,322 regular derivatives and 58 explicit compounds.
- 256 verbs; 13,824 finite forms presented separately.
- Every definition appears in the French source index; extra cross-references do not inflate lemma counts. The English edition translates that material.
- The original 465 rows, categories, meanings and valencies are retained unchanged in the source corpus.

## Editorial decisions

Additions are labelled **proposed extension**, without claiming the creator validated every entry. Grammar and phonology remain those of 1.0.0. New nouns registered as lexical units must not be assigned improvised etymologies. Transparent compounds are hyphenated; derivatives identify their base and rule.

New lexical homographs were rejected during construction. Homographs between inflections and lemmas are listed rather than hidden: 105 form/base pairs in the delivered check. Fourteen possible derivatives were rejected because their forms were already occupied. No pre-existing entry was replaced to make room.

## Checks and limitations

The check verifies preservation of entries, uniqueness, spelling of additions, infinitives, reconstruction of derivatives, compounds, reverse-index coverage and links. It does not replace exhaustive linguistic review. Examples are illustrative; naturalness, idiom and perception in singing remain to be developed. No audio was downloaded or generated.

A precautionary copy of replaced files is retained outside the active notes. It is not an archive of Suno comments.

See [Coverage and limits](11_Couverture_et_limites.md) for open domains and `controle_coherence.json` for the checks actually executed.

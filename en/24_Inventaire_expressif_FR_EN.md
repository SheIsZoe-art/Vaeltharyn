## Coverage and editorial method

The [unaltered supplied text](../registres/inventaire-2026-10-25/references/inventaire-fourni.txt), [provenance hash](../registres/inventaire-2026-10-25/references/provenance.json), [full coverage table](../registres/inventaire-2026-10-25/couverture.csv), [authored routing](../registres/inventaire-2026-10-25/couverture-redigee.txt), and [sense data](../registres/inventaire-2026-10-25/sens.json) preserve the distinction between the supplied correspondences and our checked proposals. Every supplied row remains visible, including repetitions and uncertain items. Definitions and valencies were read for reused lexical material; this is not a claim to have manually validated the full 65,000-word dictionary.

American *pissed* as anger is distinguished from British intoxication; *badass* has an admiring and an intimidating reading. *Jobard* is corrected towards gullibility; *knucklehead* does not require stubbornness. A stupid mistake is not necessarily bullshit, a sexual act is not the same speech act as dismissal, and an intensifier is not automatically a negative quality judgement.

Four rows have no established native equivalent: two unverified regional forms, “D jobi / jobi” and “Gognand”, and two culturally specific class/racialised labels, “trailer trash” and “white trash”. They are documented with their limitations instead of being given a fabricated fictional history. Other partial pragmatic or cultural transfers are labelled in the sense notes.

Sexual and gendered abuse is recorded as language used by a speaker, not evidence of real conduct, orientation or consent. No grammatical gender is introduced; existing human nouns can retain an explicitly female or male referent where needed. Clinical diagnoses are not inferred from invective. Being a sex worker, sexually active, physically weak or single is not automatically translated as being malicious or criminal.

## Written consistency and sound

The nine new lemmas are defined denominals or noun compounds built from existing lexical material, not randomly allocated roots. Root stress, compound secondary stress and unchanged concatenation apply. Long compounds are available expressive choices, not mandatory rap hooks. The checks cover collisions, lexical analyses, pronouns and inflection, not recorded delivery or acoustic naturalness.

Subjects and targets are retained: “he is mocking me” is not silently turned into a question addressed to “you”. The source phrase about being given “les boules” has separate irritation and fear readings. Negative controls using a music verb for creating a quarrel, `sha` before a noun or `ko` as “like” are excluded from translations and retained only as test fixtures.

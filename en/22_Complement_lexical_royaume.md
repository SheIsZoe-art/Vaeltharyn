## VX-ba7775df8b38f870

**foelthor-dhem** · `n` · **English:** greenhouse

A structure with light-transmitting walls used to shelter cultivated plants and control their growing conditions; a horticultural greenhouse, not a bird's talon or an act of squeezing.

**Valency:** not applicable. **Register:** neutral.

**Formation:** modifier + head noun compound; only the final element is inflected.

**Example:** En kaerun en foelthor-dhemen vele vaethun. — The greenhouse door is open.

**Status:** proposed supplement of 2026-10-24; added at the creator's request. **Source:** Vaeltharyn project creation at the creator's request, 2026-10-24.

## VX-ed691156e48fb76d

**garuniv** · `adj` · **English:** damn, colloquial intensifier

A colloquial emotional intensifier accompanying a noun, expressing excitement, wonder or irritation; the expressive sense of damn in whole damn night, without religious condemnation.

**Valency:** not applicable. **Register:** colloquial, expressive.

**Formation:** invented root; no historical etymology is claimed.

**Example:** En nyr verys garuniv. — The whole damn night.

**Status:** proposed supplement of 2026-10-24; added at the creator's request. **Source:** Vaeltharyn project creation at the creator's request, 2026-10-24.

## VX-74a0af6f326db352

**haedhoefovar** · `v` · **English:** to need

To feel or have a requirement for a thing or an action; not merely to want it.

**Valency:** transitive/modal: a nominal direct object or an infinitive sharing the same subject; dhe + clause when the subjects differ. **Register:** neutral.

**Formation:** noun + ar, a lexicalised derivation with explicitly registered transitive/modal valency; this valency is not a general rule for denominal verbs.

**Example:** Mi haedhoefovem velar sulinun. — I need to be saved.

**Status:** proposed supplement of 2026-10-24; added at the creator's request. **Source:** Vaeltharyn project creation at the creator's request, 2026-10-24.

## VX-40068b8d4eaea8cf

**raelun-fodhath** · `n` · **English:** sauna

A dry-heat bath taken in a strongly heated cabin; by explicitly accepted metonymy, the cabin where it is taken.

**Valency:** not applicable. **Register:** neutral.

**Formation:** lexicalised noun compound; the precise meaning sauna is registered, not inferred from every kind of heat bath.

**Example:** En raelun-fodhath vele nufaedhor. — The sauna is hot.

## VX-100e0e43afdeebaa

**thimraenar** · `v` · **English:** to reach towards

To move or extend one's hand towards a person or thing in order to reach or grasp it; the target is the direct object.

**Formation:** invented root; no historical etymology is claimed.

**Example:** Mi thimraenem en thim ti-en. — I reach towards your hand.

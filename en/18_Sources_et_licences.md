# Sources, licences and attribution

## French source definitions and their English translations

The new source definitions come from **contributors to the French Wiktionary**, through structured **Kaikki / Wiktextract** data:

- [French-edition overview and licences](https://kaikki.org/frwiktionary/).
- [French dictionary and download link](https://kaikki.org/frwiktionary/Fran%C3%A7ais/index.html).
- [JSONL source used](https://kaikki.org/frwiktionary/Fran%C3%A7ais/kaikki.org-dictionary-Fran%C3%A7ais.jsonl).
- [Wiktionary reuse policy](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Copyrights).

Kaikki declares availability under the same licences as Wiktionary: **CC BY-SA and GFDL**. Reused definition text remains subject to its source licence conditions. English translations are adaptations of the credited French definitions; they do not erase their provenance. Bulk English translations were produced locally with the Argos French–English 1.9 model and are not individually reviewed.

Each new entry supplies its Wiktionary URL and the sense number used in the extraction. The source page provides access to history and contributors; numbering may change after collection. `Grand dictionnaire/definitions_sourcees.jsonl` preserves the exact French definition text used and its attribution. The English detailed CSV also retains French headwords and source definitions. Keep these references when exporting or sharing the dictionary.

Original transformations: lemma/category filtering, selection of one usable sense per proposal, removal of quotations and author examples, addition of a Vaeltharyn form, calculated pronunciation, domain and editorial metadata. French grammatical gender labels do not create grammatical gender in Vaeltharyn. Proper names, media, recordings and illustrations were not imported. The English edition adds translations, language navigation and English-sorted lookup indexes, while keeping canonical forms and IDs unchanged.

The collection read 95,000 candidate lemma/category records from part of the source file; **it is not a complete mirror of Wiktionary**. The final selection is not a Larousse word list. Larousse is mentioned solely as a scale comparison: [2027 edition, 64,650 words announced](https://www.hachette.fr/livre/petit-larousse-illustre-2027-9782036076570/). No Larousse definitions were copied.

## Vaeltharyn forms

New roots are inventions proposed for this project. They are neither official translations nor attested historical-language words. Automatic allocation follows Vaeltharyn sounds and syllable structures and avoids the checked collisions. It establishes neither the absence of accidental resemblance to a real language nor the poetic quality of a word.

## Earlier material

The 2,918 entries installed before the comprehensive extension retain their earlier provenance, proposals and rules; do not reattribute them to Wiktionary. Workshop examples are original project proposals, distinct from reused definitions. Their English versions translate those examples without changing the canonical Vaeltharyn text.

## Public release of the project

Since 24 October 2026, original project material has been offered under **CC BY-SA 4.0** by its rights holder. This supplements the earlier status of original resources; it does not replace the rights or attribution requirements of third-party definitions. See [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

# Vaeltharyn — English edition

[Français](../README.md) · **English**

**A constructed language for singing, poetry and storytelling.**

Pronunciation: /ˈvæːl.θa.ryn/. Vaeltharyn has a productive grammar, regular conjugation and a bidirectional lexicon. Its sound evokes Nordic and Celtic inspirations; its grammar and roots are creations of this project.

> **Working edition 1.2.0, including the lexical supplement of 24 October 2026.** The current data contains **65,007 entries**, **65,006 distinct spellings** and **6,533 verbs**. The comprehensive supplement includes 62,083 automatically allocated lexical proposals that have not all been individually reviewed.

## Translation and review status

This edition translates the full documentation and lexical material into English. Pronunciation, grammar, conjugation, word formation and the song guide were directly translated and checked against the French reference. Other chapters and the bulk lexical definitions use local machine translation with structural checks; they have **not** all been individually reviewed. The English text is a working translation, not a new validation of every lexical proposal.

Canonical Vaeltharyn words, identifiers, paradigms, song text, IPA and source URLs are retained. French headwords, source definitions and historical reports are deliberately preserved as provenance data. They allow comparison with the [French edition](../README.md), which remains the reference when a translation is ambiguous. Internal filenames are shared between the editions to keep their correspondence stable.

# Examples and meaning distinctions

Back: [Overview](00_Accueil.md). Written examples from extension 1.1.0; no audio validation. These sentences are linguistic examples, not factual assertions or actions performed.

| Vaeltharyn | English | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| Mi vaerem en maelvel ti-or. | I give you the bread. | Direct object followed by the dative beneficiary. |
| Mi maelvelem en pelvar. | I eat the apple. | Inherited verb maelvelar; new object pelvar. |
| Se vathune en vath. | They drink the water (singular). | vathunar → vathune. |
| Mi mirem en naelmir. | I see the screen. | Computer screen, not a protective screen. |
| En thaemnyr vele thiriv. | The silence is temporary. | Acoustic silence; invariable adjective. |
| En thaelraun vele naelv. | The chorus is memorable. | Musical noun; copula velar. |
| Min thaerom kois. | We sing together. | First-person plural verb agreement. |
| Mi thaemnaelem en thael. | I record the song. | thaemnaelar, transitive. |
| Mi thaemvaerem en thaemnael. | I mix the sound recording. | Action and object defined within the same domain. |
| Mi naelgraelem en naelneli. | I back up the digital data. | naelneli is a collective noun in the lexical singular. |
| Mi nu naeldhelem en thaemnael. | I do not download the sound recording. | Negation nu; an illustrative sentence, no action performed. |
| Ti sendles en vaeril ma ? | Are you learning the language? | Final question particle; sendlar → sendles. |
| Mi sendraerem en tharelvel. | I understand the sentence. | Direct object of a transitive verb. |
| Se vele en sendvarun. | They are the teacher (singular). | Role noun without grammatical gender. |
| Mi ravaermarem en naelvel. | I bought the book. | ra- + vaermar + em. |
| Se ravele thuriv; ithir se vele haeviv. | They were ill; now they are healthy (singular). | Neutral past followed by present. |
| En haevkor vele dholiv. | The medicine is necessary. | Linguistic example, not an actual prescription. |
| En maelnayrun mi-en vele vaethv. | My spouse is brave. | Postposed possessive; adjective without agreement. |
| Mi thaemsaerem en varnelven. | I greet the neighbour. | Action directed towards a person. |
| Mi maerunem en lyrenraun ti-en. | I respect your personal boundary. | Transitive verb; postposed possessive. |
| En saelhael vele haevvaer. | Empathy is useful. | saelhael is not the blessing saelhaer. |
| En naelkaen vele naiv. | The computer program is new. | naelkaen is not naelkaer, concentration. |
| En vaermar vele e en vaerdhemath. | The money is in the bank. | Spatial preposition and locative -ath. |
| En vaurkor vele haervenun. | Gold is heavy. | Gold the metal, distinct from money. |
| En lyth vele e en vaemath. | The tongue is in the mouth. | lyth is the organ; vaeril is a language system. |
| En thaem vele thaemhyriv. | The voice is low-pitched. | Musical register, not seriousness or severity. |
| Mi saerem to sendhemath. | I go towards the school. | to + locative, including movement. |
| Se vithaere e en thaemthavath. | They will sing in the studio (singular). | Future vi- and a location complement. |
| Mi thaerem en nyr-thael. | I sing the night-song. | Noun + noun compound. |
| Mi mirem en nyr-thaeli. | I see the night-songs. | Only the final compound element takes the plural. |
| Ae min thaerirom kois. | May we sing together. | Wish ae + subjunctive 1PL. |
| Ir ti thaerires, mi thaerelem. | If you sang, I would sing. | Subjunctive in the condition; conditional in the main clause. |

## Translation traps

The French source uses some single words for several unrelated meanings. English equivalents below identify the intended senses rather than transferring that ambiguity into Vaeltharyn.

- **Language / tongue:** `vaeril`, linguistic system; `lyth`, bodily organ.
- **Silver / money:** `lymkor`, the metal silver; `vaermar`, money; `vaervar`, price; `vaerthur`, cost.
- **Fly / steal:** `nythalar`, move through the air; `vaervyrar`, steal.
- **Flow / sink:** `vathar`, flow; `thimdhorar`, sink into a liquid.
- **Low-pitched / serious:** `thaemhyriv` refers only to sound register; it does not automatically extend to danger or seriousness.
- **Time / climate / weather:** `thir`, time; `raelthir`, climate; `saunrael`, weather.
- **Memory:** `nael`, mental memory; `naellym`, computer RAM; `naelkorun`, storage.
- **Voice / sound / music:** `thaem`, `thaemvath`, `lyrmael`; these nouns are not synonyms.
- **Consent:** `thaevlyren`; trust `dhael` and love `mael` do not replace it.
- **Song / act of singing:** `thael`, a piece of music; `thaereth`, a process. An action nominalisation does not automatically name its result.

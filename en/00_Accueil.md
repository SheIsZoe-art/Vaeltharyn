# Vaeltharyn — Language reference

**Version 1.2.0, with the lexical supplement of 24 October 2026.**

A constructed language for deep, sublime and mystical singing, with Nordic and Celtic evocations. Its grammar and roots were created for this project. It is not Old Norse, Icelandic, Gaelic or a supposed historical Viking language. Short words may accidentally resemble words in existing languages; inventing a language cannot guarantee that every syllable is globally unprecedented.

**Vaeltharyn** is the provisional name of the language. Pronunciation: /ˈvæːl.θa.ryn/. In the initial canon, the name is an indivisible proper noun; no invented etymology is assigned to it.

Version 1 provides a productive grammar, regular conjugation, an initial bidirectional lexicon and rules for forming missing words. “Complete” here means that the main mechanisms needed for writing are defined. No living-language dictionary is ever definitively finished. Future additions must follow the rules and be recorded.

## Sound identity

Long vowels carry the singing; /m n ŋ l r v ð/ connect syllables. Clusters /sk θr vr/ provide contrast without making every word difficult to articulate. Diphthongs and open endings alternate with shorter, more percussive words. Depth, beauty and a mystical quality also depend on melody, performance and arrangement; an alphabet alone does not guarantee them.

## First sentence

**Mi thaerem; aelun vele e mi-en saelath.**

I sing; the light remains in my soul.

`mi`, I · `thaer-em`, sing-present.1SG · `aelun`, light · `vel-e`, be-present.3SG · `e`, in · `mi-en`, of me/my · `sael-ath`, soul-locative. In `mi-en saelath`, the pronominal possessive may precede the noun under the special rule in the grammar.

The historical comprehensive edition has **65,000 distinct lexical spellings**, 65,001 lemma/category entries and 6,531 verbs. It retains the 2,918 earlier entries and adds 62,083 defined, sourced proposals. Conjugated forms do not inflate the count. Its numerical scale is comparable to a general dictionary; individual editorial review of the supplement remains outstanding.

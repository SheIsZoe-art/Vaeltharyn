## Work completed

The supplement adds 62,083 proposals, each with **one defined French sense**, category, domain, source register, stable form, working syllable segmentation and calculated IPA. Plurals and conjugations are not multiplied to reach the total. The earlier 2,918 entries and their original columns are preserved in the source data.

New forms were allocated automatically from a syllable inventory compatible with the phonology. The system reserves lemmas, regular inflections and several simple derivatives; 5,423 candidates were rejected during allocation to avoid collisions. This does not resolve every inherited syntactic ambiguity or derivations of arbitrary depth.

Direct and reverse dictionaries access the same entries. Batch links and anchors were checked. The supplement supplies all regular paradigms of new verbs; earlier tables remain available. French words already linked to a known entry are search cross-references, without changing its existing Vaeltharyn meaning.

## Editorial work remaining

**The 62,083 new proposals have not been individually reviewed as the articles of an edited published dictionary would be.** Source filters may admit questionable definitions, duplicated senses or irrelevant terms. Forms are phonologically checked; their aesthetic quality and actual ease of singing are not demonstrated.

Etymological and morphological families have not been reconstructed for all new stems. An automatically allocated root does not claim descent from an old word. New units still need comparison and differentiation when used domains are reviewed. French determines neither Vaeltharyn culture nor obligatory metaphors.

A definite noun phrase or first-person form is a **morphological example**, not evidence of natural usage. Not all entries have contextual sentences, reviewed synonyms, antonyms or collocations. The workshop provides song starting points; forms selected for each song need review with their translation.

No audio test, music download or change to existing songs was performed. This review claims no absolute exhaustiveness of French, scientific terminology or possible expressions. The English bulk translation adds a further review requirement, documented in [Translation report](TRANSLATION.md).

## Versioning

This is compatible extension 1.2.0: no grammar rule changes and no earlier forms are removed. Later corrections must retain IDs, record old and new forms, explain the change and search song occurrences before migration.

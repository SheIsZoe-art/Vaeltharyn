# Lexical workshop for a song series

Back: [Comprehensive dictionary](15_Grand_dictionnaire.md). These palettes provide routes into the dictionary, not compulsory lyrics or decisions about characters. New sourced meanings remain proposals. English table definitions are working machine translations; read the linked entry and French source when needed.

## Choose by meaning and sound

Start with the message, then choose a concrete image and a tension: a shore left behind, a closed window, a returning voice. A list of mystical words does not replace a scene. Then check stress, syllables and whether vowels can be sustained over the melody. The dictionary imposes no required rhyme.

Each chorus should be able to repeat a few stable words. Across the series, keep a small motif lexicon with the chosen meanings and spellings: a flame should not become a crystal in the next song just for a rhyme. Characters, relationships and worlds remain those decided by the creator.

## Distinctions to retain throughout the songs

- `mael`, love; `vael`, desire or will; `thaevlyren`, consent. None replaces the others.
- `nael`, mental memory; `naellym`, computer memory; `maelkor`, a keepsake. Metaphors must remain identifiable.
- `thaem`, voice; `thael`, song; `thaerineth`, melody; `lyrmael`, music; `thaemnyr`, acoustic silence.
- `nyrel`, a sleeping dream; `maev`, imagination; `thrael`, hope. A desired project is not automatically a nocturnal dream.
- `sael`, soul or inner life; `saelun`, identity; `haelun`, consciousness. Their connection may be poetic, but they are distinct nouns.
- `vathar`, flow; `thimdhorar`, sink. `nythalar`, fly through the air; `vaervyrar`, steal.
- `graeth`, oath; `lyren`, bond; `kaelun`, boundary or limit. A consensual relationship does not imply confinement.

## Before selecting a new word

1. Read the whole definition, not only its French or English lookup label.
2. Check category, valency and register; do not import all senses of a French or English word.
3. Write a short translated sentence and check its cases and agreement.
4. Record the word and meaning in the song notes with version 1.2.0.
5. If a phonetic realisation is tested in Suno, keep it beside the canonical spelling.
6. For a lexical correction, record the equivalence and affected songs before changing a form.

## Continuity of the first song

Fra en thyrath - Hors de l’ombre (Out of the shadow) retains all its original lyrics and translations. Expanding the dictionary does not authorise rewriting the song. Its roots come from the initial core and remain available.

# Canon and checks

## Status

Version 1.0.0 was created on 24 October 2026 for an original language evoking Nordic and Celtic sounds, with deep, mystical singing in mind. The name and initial canon are proposals to the user; subsequent corrections are versioned. This is not a reconstructed historical language. Originality concerns the created system and organised lexicon; no claim excludes every accidental resemblance to an existing language or fictional work.

## Basic decisions

- Latin spelling; digraphs th/dh/ng/ae/oe; explicitly marked long vowels.
- Stable stress on the first syllable of the root.
- No grammatical gender or initial mutation.
- Nouns: root + plural i + case; five cases.
- Verbs: infinitive in ar; six persons, three tenses and three finite moods.
- Aspect particles; passive with velar and the participle in un.
- SVO order; VSO only after ke.
- Negation nu; final question particle ma; relatives with ya.
- Nominal possessors follow the noun; pronominal possessors may precede or follow it.
- Word creation through declared derivation and compounding rules.

## Initial-version limitations

The lexicon provides a general and poetic foundation, not complete coverage of medicine, law or computing. Cultural idioms, dialects and fictional history are not invented as if they were attested. No dedicated runic alphabet has yet been defined: randomly using historical runes would blur the project's identity.

Sentences and the song were checked in writing. Whether they sound sublime or mystical must be judged by listening. No audio test is presented as completed.

## Accepted ambiguities

`thar` = big (adjective) or because (conjunction). `pel` = decimal separator, classified with objects in the initial arrangement although its function is grammatical. `sen` = plural pronoun or the preposition without. `un/nul` = numbers and determiners. These uses are identified, not translations that change with the lyrics.

A noun and an inflected form may be homographs. For example, `varen`, river, and `var-en`, of whom, have the same unsegmented written form if the interrogative is written without segmentation. Nominal interrogatives retain their interrogative context; morphological annotation may separate morphemes. Rephrase especially ambiguous passages.

## Versioning

- 1.0.x: typo correction or clarification without a form change.
- 1.x.0: compatible additions of words, examples or constructions.
- 2.0.0: a change to an already used rule or form; provide equivalences and migrate examples.

A validated decision takes precedence over later improvisation. Check a new text against the dictionary; do not use it to silently reinvent root meanings.

## Delivered checks

`controle_coherence.json` describes mechanical checks of entries, verb forms, Obsidian links and words in the song. Such checks identify structural inconsistencies; they establish neither global word originality nor musical quality.

## Lexical extension 1.1.0 — 2026-10-24

At the creator's request, the dictionary was expanded to 2,918 entries, including 465 unchanged inherited entries. Additions remain lexical proposals and do not imply individual validation. The rules of 1.0.0 remain in force. See [Extension journal](13_Journal_extension_1_1.md), [Dictionary guide](10_Guide_du_dictionnaire.md) and [Coverage and limits](11_Couverture_et_limites.md).

The check report is updated only for operations actually rerun. It also lists homographs between inflections and lemmas; it does not claim that all ambiguity is eliminated. Earlier song-recognition results are not presented as a new 1.1.0 check.

## Comprehensive edition 1.2.0 — 2026-10-24

The creator requested the scale of a general dictionary for a song series. Delivered: 65,000 distinct spellings and 65,001 entries, preserving the earlier 2,918 entries. This supplement is a proposed working lexicon: forms were allocated automatically and definitions selected from an open source. It must not be described as individually reviewed in full. Existing songs are unchanged. See [Comprehensive dictionary](15_Grand_dictionnaire.md), [Editorial review](21_Bilan_grande_edition.md) and [Sources and licences](18_Sources_et_licences.md).

Word formation, case and conjugation rules are unchanged. Generation checks collisions with reserved lemmas, inflections and simple derivatives; it proves neither naturalness nor the absence of resemblance to every real language. Later corrections retain IDs, document previous forms and check songs using them.

## Targeted lexical supplement — 2026-10-24

At the creator's explicit request, six entries needed for a song translation were added without changing grammar or replacing existing forms. See [The supplement](22_Complement_lexical_royaume.md) for definitions, valencies, status and check limitations. Verb haedhoefovar is explicitly lexicalised with transitive/modal valency; this does not generalise to every denominal verb. Existing rurithar is retained for to lean on; no redundant synonym is added. Existing songs are unchanged.

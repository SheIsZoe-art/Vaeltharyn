**Reading:**  `dae·toen`; emphasis on the first syllable of the radical.

**Source of definition:**  [Wiktionary — cæcum](https://fr.wiktionary.org/wiki/c%C3%A6cum#fr), source sense 1; Wiktionnaire contributors, Kaikki extraction. [Licence and award](../../../../../docs/en/reference/sources-and-licenses.md).

**Status:** lexical proposal 1.2.0; mechanical control performed.

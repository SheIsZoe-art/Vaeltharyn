# Sources and licenses

Original Vaeltharyn material follows [LICENSE.md](../../../LICENSE.md). [references.json](../../../data/provenance/references.json) resolves attribution codes used by records.

French definitions by Wiktionary contributors, via Kaikki / Wiktextract, retain their attribution and the licenses declared by that source. Per-entry URLs and sense numbers are preserved. English translations do not erase this attribution. `data/provenance/definitions-sourcees.jsonl` retains the French definition text used.

- [French dataset and licensing](https://kaikki.org/frwiktionary/).
- [Wiktionary reuse conditions](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Copyrights).
- [CC BY-SA 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Lexical and technical sources support sense distinctions; they do not automatically approve proposed Vaeltharyn forms. Individual record status applies. Retain credits and source references in exports.

# Neutral grammar examples

| Vaeltharyn | English | Grammatical point |
|---|---|---|
| Mi thaerem. | I sing. | present 1SG |
| Ti rathaeres. | You sang / were singing / have sung. | neutral past |
| En saeli vithaeren. | The souls will sing. | plural + future 3PL |
| Mi dha rathaerem. | I had sung. | completed past |
| Mi ela nu thaerem. | I am not singing. | aspect + negation |
| Mi vaelem thaerar. | I want to sing. | modal + infinitive |
| Ti thaeres ma ? | Do you sing? | yes/no question |
| Var thaere ? | Who sings? | subject question |
| Mi vaerem en thael ti-or. | I give you the song. | object + dative |
| Aelun vele e mi-en saelath. | The light is in my soul. | pronominal possession + location |
| Mi thaerem thaemul. | I sing by means of the voice. | instrumental |
| Mi sendem dhe ti thaeres. | I know that you sing. | complement clause |
| En thael ya mi thaerem vele saev. | The song that I sing is beautiful. | object relative |
| En thael vele thaerun fra mi-ath. | The song is sung by me. | passive |
| Ir ti thaerires, mi thaerelem. | If you sang, I would sing. | unreal condition |
| Ae en saeli thaeriren. | May the souls sing. | wish + subjunctive |
| Ke thaere en sael. | The soul sings, with ritual emphasis. | marked VSO |
| La min thaerom kois. | Let us sing together. | hortative |
| Mi neravem se. | I make them sleep (singular). | causative |
| En nauri aelunen. | The fires shine. | plural nominal subject |

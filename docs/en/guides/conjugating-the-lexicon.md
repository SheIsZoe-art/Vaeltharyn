# Conjugating the comprehensive lexicon

Back: [Comprehensive dictionary](dictionary-editions.md). The rules in [Conjugation](../reference/conjugation.md) are unchanged. An infinitive always ends in `-ar`: remove exactly those two letters, then add mood and person markers. New verbs have two or three root syllables, stressed on the first.

The historical comprehensive dictionary contains **6,531 verbs**. The 256 earlier paradigms remain in [Verb paradigms](../reference/verb-paradigms.md). The following tables cover new forms: 54 finite forms per verb, or **338,850 forms**. These are not additional lexical entries. The two verbs in the later supplement bring the current total to 6,533 and follow the same rules; they are documented in [the supplement](../extensions/kingdom.md).

The labels `tr` and `intr` are taken from available usage labels for the selected source sense. A French pronominal label alone does not determine the Vaeltharyn construction: do not mechanically add `si`. When only a pronominal label was available, the predicate was proposed as intransitive and its construction requires review before use. Senses requiring an undefined indirect construction were excluded from the supplement. A passive participle is proposed only with a transitive reading.

# Discover Vaeltharyn

Vaeltharyn is a constructed language by **SheIsZoe**, intended for deep, mystical singing with Nordic and Celtic associations. It is not a historical language. Its indivisible proper name is pronounced /ˈvæːl.θa.ryn/.

Productive grammar, conjugation and word formation support writing beyond recorded lemmas while distinguishing proposals from approved forms. A dictionary remains open-ended; its size does not prove that every entry has been individually reviewed.

Use [the English guide](README.md) to navigate, [lookup](integration/lookup.md) for current records and [grammar](reference/grammar.md) to construct sentences. Historical editions are archived and do not replace current lookup.

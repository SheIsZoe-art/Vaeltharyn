# Contributing to Vaeltharyn

Corrections and proposals are welcome. Open an issue to report a problem or discuss an idea; submit a pull request for a specific change.

## Report or correct an entry

Identify the file, lexical ID when available, form, problem and proposed correction. For a translation, include the intended meaning and a contextual sentence. Compare English corrections with the French source definition; do not broaden a word to every sense of its nearest English equivalent.

## Propose a word or rule

Specify the meaning, grammatical category, bases and formation, pronunciation and a translated example. Check the grammar, homographs and inflected forms before inventing a root. Mark new material as a **proposal** until validated by the project maintainer.

## Preserve the corpus

Keep stable IDs, document old forms and changes in the version history, and preserve sources and licences. A lexical correction must not silently rewrite existing texts. Historical dictionary volumes and current CSVs have different scopes.

The comprehensive supplement and English bulk translation have not been individually reviewed in full. Prefer documented, targeted corrections over mass replacements. Use relative Markdown links for GitHub navigation. Keep French and English entries aligned by ID.

## Rights

Add only material you may share under the repository's terms. Original contributions are offered under CC BY-SA 4.0; third-party material must identify its source, licence and required attribution.

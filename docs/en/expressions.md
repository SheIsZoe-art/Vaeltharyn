# Expressions and sense distinctions

The reference contains 79 register expressions, 226 sense records and 111 sound/body constructions and 40 emotion/dialogue constructions. Keys and IDs identify senses; two senses may share one wording without being merged.

Neutral aliases in `data/expressions/aliases.json` connect a term and language to one or more records. They are not universal equivalences: consider addressee, subject, intent and intensity.

Inspect complete records with `python -m vaeltharyn --lang en "badass" --full`. Admiration and threat are distinct; *pissed* can concern anger or intoxication; adjectival *shit-faced* concerns intoxication. Do not invent fictional populations to transpose culturally marked abuse.

Technical constructions distinguish physical frequency, pitch and timbre; a pad sound and physical pad; musical keyboard and key ring; anatomical waist and cutting. Retain model names `TR-606` and `TR-808`. A metaphor does not change physical word meanings.

Records marked `schema_a_completer` contain slots: recover contextual antecedents, actors and objects. Preserve the scope of *only*. *Unforgotten* does not automatically mean *unforgettable*.

Register and intensity range from mild exclamation to severe insult. Sarcasm may depend on delivery. Senses without an established equivalent remain explicitly uncertain.

See [Emotions, gestures and dialogue](guides/emotions-and-dialogue.md) for formations, examples and sense distinctions.
